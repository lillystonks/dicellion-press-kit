Roll dice across a glassy grid, tip each one onto a new face, and line up matches: two 2s, three 3s, four 4s, all the way up to six 6s.

A group doesn't vanish straight away. It sinks into the board, and every matching die you cram in before it goes fuses into a bigger score. Clear again quickly to build a chain multiplier. Ones never score, so they have to be rolled out of the way.

Inspired by the cult PlayStation classic Devil Dice, Dicellion is the kind of game you can learn in a minute, take a weekend to master, and still be chasing one more chain long after you meant to stop.

Short description, ready to paste

Dicellion is a fast, fizzing dice puzzler from UK solo developer Retro Spect. Roll dice onto matching faces, fuse sinking groups into huge scores, and chain clears across five themed stages, endless Unlimited mode and local head-to-head. Out on Steam for Windows and macOS on 30 November 2026, with a free demo available now.

The game

The one rule: a group of N dice showing N scores.

Start

Five stages, five themes. Each hands you a checklist of groups to make, and clearing it lets you keep that stage's theme and soundtrack.

Unlimited

Pure score attack. The dice keep coming, the board keeps filling and the levels keep climbing.

Multiplayer

Local head-to-head against a friend or the bot (Easy, Normal or Difficult). Share one board and steal each other's groups, or race on a board each. Three, five or ten minute matches.

  • Four bonuses earned with special dice: Bomb clears the board, Freeze holds back new dice, Joker doubles points, Sweep explodes the commonest die.
  • Six themes, each with its own dice and music. One is a secret, unlocked by earning every achievement.
  • 25 Steam achievements and leaderboards for every kind of match.
  • Keyboard or controller, with Steam Input support. Menus also work with a mouse.
  • Accessibility options: high-contrast dice, adjustable roll speed, keyboard-only play.
  • Fully translated into English plus 28 more languages.
