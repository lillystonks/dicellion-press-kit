DICELLION ROLLS ONTO STEAM ON 30 NOVEMBER
A fast, fizzing dice puzzler from UK solo developer Retro Spect.
Free demo available now.

For immediate release

Retro Spect today confirmed that Dicellion, a puzzle game about tumbling dice
into place, launches on Steam for Windows and macOS on 30 November 2026,
priced $6.99 USD with 20% off for launch week. A free demo is out now.

Players roll dice across a glassy grid, tipping each one onto a new face, and
line up matches: two 2s, three 3s, four 4s, all the way up to six 6s. A group
does not vanish straight away. It sinks into the board, and every matching die
squeezed in before it goes fuses into a bigger score. Clear again quickly to
build a chain multiplier. Ones never score, so they have to be rolled out of
the way.

Inspired by the cult PlayStation classic Devil Dice, Dicellion is the kind of
game you can learn in a minute, take a weekend to master, and still be
chasing one more chain long after you meant to stop.

Three ways to play
- Start: five stages, five themes. Each hands you a checklist of groups to
  make, and clearing it lets you keep that stage's theme and soundtrack.
- Unlimited: pure score attack, as the dice keep coming and the levels keep
  climbing.
- Multiplayer: local head-to-head against a friend or a bot (Easy, Normal or
  Difficult). Share one board and steal each other's groups, or race on a
  board each. Three, five or ten minute matches.

Also included
- Four bonuses earned by scoring special dice: Bomb, Freeze, Joker and Sweep.
- Six themes, each with its own dice and music. One is a secret, unlocked by
  earning every achievement.
- 25 Steam achievements, leaderboards for every kind of match, Steam Cloud
  saves.
- Play on keyboard or controller, with Steam Input support. The menus also
  work with a mouse.
- English plus 28 more languages.

"Retro Spect is a one-person indie developer, based in the UK, fuelled by far
too much coffee and a passion for nostalgic games."
